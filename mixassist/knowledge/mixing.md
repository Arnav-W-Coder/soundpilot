## Mixing
Ranges (Hz): kick 50-100 punch, 2k-5k click; bass 40-250; snare 150-250 body, 5k crack; hats 6k-12k; vocal 100-300 body, 2k-5k presence, 5k-8k sibilance, 10k+ air; guitar 80-250 body, 1k-4k bite.
Problems: boomy 60-150, mud 200-500, boxy 300-600, harsh 2k-5k, sibilant 5k-8k.
Levels (guidance only): track peaks ~-6 dBFS; master true peak <= -1 dBTP; streaming ~-14 LUFS integrated, loud genres -9 to -8.
Pan: kick, bass, sub, snare, lead vocal at 0; hats/perc 10-40, doubles 60-100 as L/R pairs, pads wide. Low end stays mono: never widen or pan kick, bass or sub.
Moves: cut before boost, 1-3 dB steps (rarely > 6), 1-2 changes at a time; volume_change_db unless the user names a level. Bring a part forward by cutting what competes with it.
Built-in EQ: low shelf, mid bell, high shelf, no Q. The tools have no high-pass, notch, compressor or sidechain: say so and suggest an FL plugin.
Fixes: kick vs bass -> Bass mid -2..-3 dB at 60-100 Hz or one of them 1-2 dB down, suggest sidechain; vocal buried -> competitors mid -2..-3 dB near 3k or Vocal +1..+2 dB; mud -> mid -3 dB near 300 Hz on pads/guitars/bass; harsh -> mid -2..-3 dB near 3.5k.
